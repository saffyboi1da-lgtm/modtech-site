Refund Policy

Last updated [[SET the date on the day this is published]].

If ModTech isn't right for you, contact us within 14 calendar days of your first payment for a full refund of that first payment under our first-purchase guarantee. You don't need to give a reason. We use the date you contact us to assess eligibility. This guarantee is our own offer, on top of your legal rights.

Our voluntary first-purchase guarantee is available once to each purchaser: the individual or business buying the subscription. Creating another account does not renew eligibility. This restriction does not affect your statutory rights.

We offer this guarantee in good faith. If it is misused, for example by buying and refunding again and again, we may decline a refund under this guarantee or end a licence, and we will tell you why by email.

How to request one

Email contact@modtech.app from the address you bought with, and include your order number. Refunds are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record, and returned to your original payment method where possible. Paddle's policy is to refund within 14 days of approving a request (and, for a statutory withdrawal, within 14 days of receiving your request). Please contact us or Paddle if you need help with a charge. This does not restrict your right to dispute a payment.

What happens to your licence

When we request a full refund of your current payment, we also cancel your subscription straight away. Editing then continues for 7 days after the cancellation is recorded (the grace period in our Terms), after which ModTech becomes read-only: you can still open your library and export your tech packs as PDF, but you cannot edit them or make new ones. Your work stays on your Mac exactly as you left it, and any PDFs you have already exported are yours to keep. We also review refunds that Paddle makes directly and arrange cancellation where appropriate. A refund on its own does not change your access; cancelling does. A refund relating to an earlier period does not automatically remove access you have paid for since. If a payment is charged back, we treat it like a full refund of that payment: we cancel your subscription and your access ends as described above.

Renewals and later requests

Renewal payments are not covered by our voluntary first-purchase guarantee. If a renewal was unexpected, contact us promptly; we consider goodwill requests individually. Paddle may also issue refunds under its own terms, and nothing here limits your statutory rights.

Cancelling is not a refund

You can cancel at any time through Paddle's customer portal, or email us and we will cancel it for you. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access for the period you have paid for plus the 7-day grace in our Terms. It does not refund payments you have already made. Refunding a payment and cancelling a subscription are separate actions. When we refund a payment in full we also cancel the subscription, as described above.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer protection law that cannot be excluded. The first-purchase guarantee, including its once-per-purchaser limit, is our own offer on top of those rights; it does not replace them and the limit does not apply to them. If ModTech has a major fault, you may be entitled to a refund or replacement even after 14 days, so please get in touch.
