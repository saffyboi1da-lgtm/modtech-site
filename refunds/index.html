Refund Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026.

If ModTech isn't right for you, you have 30 days from your first payment to request a full refund, no questions asked.

How to request one

Email hello@modtech.app with your license email or order number. Refunds are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record, and are typically returned to your original payment method within a few business days.

After 30 days

Renewals and requests made after the first 30 days are handled case by case — get in touch and we'll work it out. You can cancel future renewals at any time through Paddle's customer portal; cancelling stops future charges but doesn't refund past ones.
