What to know before you buy

  • Mac only. Mac with Apple Silicon. Tested on macOS 15.5; Intel Macs and other macOS versions have not yet been verified.
  • One seat is one Mac. A Mac that has not been used for 14 days can be released to make room for a new one. There is no shared team library yet: each person has their own copy of their own library.
  • Your packs stay on your Mac. ModTech goes online only to check your licence and to send feedback if you choose to.
  • If your subscription ends you keep access to the end of the period you paid for, plus 7 days (what happens after a refund is set out in the refund policy). After that ModTech becomes read-only: you can still open your library and export your tech packs as PDF.
