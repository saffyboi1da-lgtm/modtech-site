Private beta — Mac

Build tech packs from your own library. Right-click, not dig.

ModTech stores your brand's garment assets — flats, artwork, fabrics, hardware, size charts — in one searchable library, and builds the factory-ready tech pack PDF. Fill any slot by right-click from your own history. Nothing invented, nothing uploaded.

What it does

  • A searchable library for every asset your brand already has.
  • Right-click any tech-pack slot to pull from your own prior packs — no scrolling through folders.
  • Build the full factory tech-pack PDF: cover, construction, colourways, trims, hardware, artwork, POM, QC.
  • Your library and tech packs stay on your Mac. The app goes online only to check your license, look for updates, and send feedback if you choose to.

Who it's for

Independent streetwear and cut-and-sew labels who are tired of rebuilding the same tech pack from scratch every time, and tired of folders.

Status

ModTech is in private beta on Mac. We're working directly with a small number of brands before opening it up. If that's you, get in touch.
